# Privacy

_Last updated: 2026-07-07. This is a placeholder; replace it with your own policy._

## What we collect at checkout

Checkout is handled by [Stripe](https://stripe.com/privacy). To process your
purchase and issue your license, Stripe collects and shares with us:

- your **email address**,
- your **company name**,
- your **payment details** (card or other payment method), handled entirely by
  Stripe; we never see or store card numbers.

## What goes into your license

Your license file embeds your **company name** and **email** so it can be
attributed to you. It does not contain payment information.

## What we store

We keep a record of issued licenses (company, email, product, and the Stripe
session id) to support and reissue them. We do not sell your data.
